Driving & Rider Department

Driving safety. Professionalism. Performance. Practical training solutions designed to enhance safety, competence, and professionalism on and off the road.

Profile 2026

Developing safer, more competent and responsible drivers and riders.

TWSA Training & Services provides driver development, rider training, road safety, and transport workforce training solutions. We equip drivers, riders, fleet operators, and transport personnel with the knowledge, practical skills, and professional attitude required to operate safely and efficiently in today's transport environment.

Our programs focus on promoting road safety, reducing operational risks, improving fleet performance, and supporting compliance with transport regulations and industry best practices.
